FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a MACT claim?

A MACT claim is a legal claim for compensation arising from a motor vehicle accident, subject to applicable law and facts.

What if the vehicle or driver is unknown?

The appropriate legal route depends on the available police record, facts and applicable statutory scheme. Documents should be reviewed before filing.

What if an insurance company rejects the claim?

The rejection letter, policy terms and supporting records should be examined to identify the appropriate remedy.

How is compensation determined?

Compensation depends on the facts, applicable law, evidence and relevant heads of loss; there is no universal fixed amount.

Which documents should I bring?

Bring police papers, medical records, identity/family documents, vehicle and insurance records, and income evidence available with you.
