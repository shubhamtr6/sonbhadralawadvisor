FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What civil matters can be discussed?

Property, possession, partition, recovery, contract, injunction, declaration and related civil matters can be reviewed based on the documents and facts.

Can you assist with bail or criminal complaints?

Yes, relevant criminal matters can be reviewed for appropriate legal assistance, subject to the facts, documents and applicable law.

What should I bring for a consultation?

Bring the notice, FIR, court papers, agreements, property records, identity documents and other records connected with the matter, as applicable.

Can the legal route be decided without documents?

A preliminary discussion is possible, but the appropriate legal route should be confirmed after reviewing relevant facts and documents.

Is any result guaranteed?

No. Legal outcomes depend on facts, evidence, applicable law and court or authority decisions.
